One-line joke about office life for the [One Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-one-line-office-joke-24-hour-sprint-aaf86162) competition on make.fun.

## Entry

> Our 'quick sync' has lasted forty minutes, covered nothing, and somehow produced three action items for people who weren't even invited.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke — a single line, at most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. It strips surrounding whitespace and asserts the entry is one line and within 1–200 UTF-8 bytes before writing.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The entry is written to `/output/entry.txt`.
